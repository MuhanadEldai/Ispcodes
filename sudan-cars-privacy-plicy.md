# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

**Sudan Missing Cars** ("سيأرات السودان المفقودة") is a vehicle search and missing-vehicle reporting application designed to help people search for missing vehicles in Sudan and contact the person who reported a vehicle through WhatsApp.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and use only the information necessary to provide the service.

## 1. Information We Collect

Sudan Missing Cars does not require users to create an account and does not collect the user's location.

Depending on how you use the application, the following information may be stored as part of a vehicle report:

- Vehicle plate number
- Vehicle make and model
- Vehicle color
- Vehicle year
- Vehicle status
- Contact information provided for contacting the person who reported the vehicle
- Other vehicle information voluntarily provided by the person submitting a report

We do not intentionally collect passwords, contacts, precise location, or other unnecessary personal information through the application.

## 2. How We Use Information

Vehicle information is used to:

- Allow users to search for reported missing vehicles.
- Display relevant vehicle information when a matching report is found.
- Help connect people with the person who reported a vehicle.
- Maintain and manage vehicle reports.
- Update the status of vehicles when they are found.

We do not use vehicle information for unrelated purposes.

## 3. WhatsApp

The application may use WhatsApp to allow users to contact the person associated with a vehicle report or to submit a vehicle report.

When you choose to communicate through WhatsApp, the information you send is handled according to WhatsApp's own privacy practices.

You can choose whether to start a WhatsApp conversation or send information through WhatsApp.

## 4. Firebase Services and Crash Reporting

Sudan Missing Cars uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Cloud Firestore and Firebase Crashlytics.

### Firebase Cloud Firestore

Firestore is used to store and retrieve vehicle report information required for the application's functionality, such as vehicle details, report status, and contact information provided with a report.

### Firebase Crashlytics

The application uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect, diagnose, and improve application stability.

Crashlytics may automatically collect information associated with crashes and errors, including crash reports, stack traces, relevant application state, device information, operating system information, application version, and identifiers used by Firebase and Crashlytics to distinguish application installations and measure crash impact.

This information is used for crash reporting, troubleshooting, and improving the reliability and performance of the application.

We do not intentionally use Crashlytics to collect the user's precise location, contacts, passwords, or other unrelated personal information.

## 5. Advertising

The current version of Sudan Missing Cars may not contain advertising.

If advertising services such as Google AdMob are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated to accurately describe the data collected and used by the advertising service.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Vehicle report information may be processed through Firebase Firestore to provide the application's search and reporting functionality.

Information that you voluntarily send through WhatsApp is transmitted through WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's privacy practices.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored for the application's functionality.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security of information.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Vehicle reports may be retained while they are relevant to the application's purpose.

If a vehicle is found, its report may be updated to indicate that the vehicle has been recovered.

If you need a report or information associated with you to be corrected or removed, you can contact us using the contact information provided below. We will review the request and take appropriate action where applicable.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Sudan Missing Cars is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
